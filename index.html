About Us

Founded in 2021, Gaming Club is a dynamic hub for gamers, creators, and tech enthusiasts. We bridge the gap between playing games and building them. What started as a passionate community of local gamers has grown into a collaborative space offering structured hands-on courses—ranging from game design and engine development to training cutting-edge AI models for smart, adaptive gameplay. Whether you are here to compete in high-stakes gaming events or acquire future-proof tech skills, Gaming Club is where your passion meets real-world innovation.

Why Us?

  • Hands-On Tech Education: Go beyond playing. Learn game development (Unity, Unreal Engine) and modern AI training from industry-standard tools and experienced mentors.
  • Competitive & Casual Events: Join regular local tournaments, game jams, esports viewings, and networking meetups designed to challenge your skills and grow your network.
  • Proven Track Record: Operating since 2021, we have built a trusted, thriving ecosystem that consistently launches student projects and hosts memorable gaming experiences.
  • Collaborative Community: Connect with like-minded creators, software engineers, digital artists, and competitive gamers to build team projects or compete together.
  • State-of-the-Art Workstation Access: Gain access to high-performance hardware, software environments, and dedicated testing setups built specifically for gaming and heavy AI training.
